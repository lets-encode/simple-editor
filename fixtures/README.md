# Test fixtures

MEI files used by the tests: a corpus chosen for variety over volume. Every fixture keeps its own
licence, listed here with its source and attribution. Files are copied unchanged from their
sources, faults included: some are kept precisely because they are faulty (marked *pathological*).

Syntactic variants (CRLF line endings, tab indentation, single quotes, a BOM, a DOCTYPE, an
upscaled facsimile image) are generated by the tests from these files and not committed.

## le/: Let's Encode test campaigns

`score.mei` of four test campaigns at different stages of the Let's Encode pipeline, taken from the
campaign repos' history (`score@<commit>.mei`) and from in-progress task branches
(`score@<branch>-<commit>.mei`). **These are test campaigns, in progress: not correct or
authoritative in any way**, but representative of what the pipeline writes. The `piece-01/` and
`img/` layout mirrors the campaign repos' `sources/`, so the graphic targets `../img/NN.jpg` resolve
for La Espero; the other campaigns' images are not included.

- Licence: CC BY 4.0 for the encodings (each campaign's `config.yaml`); La Espero's scans, below. Campaigns of the Let's Encode project,
  mdw – University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna.
- `la-espero/`: *La Espero* (F. M. de Menil): initialised (`b787b95`, no measures), score setup
  (`ec3585b`, staves and empty measures), an OMR draft in progress (`encode-T0002`), an accepted
  encoding (`1dd5630`); with the OMR data (`omr.xml`, `layout.json`, `layout-corrected.json`) and the
  two page images. The scans in `img/` come from IMSLP (https://imslp.org/wiki/Special:ReverseLookup/193412)
  and are licensed CC BY 3.0, as stated in the images' footer.
- `beethoven-woo68/`: Beethoven, Twelve Variations WoO 68: measure correction (`c89a6ea`) and an OMR
  draft in progress (`encode-T0001`).
- `bach-bwv-846/`: J. S. Bach, BWV 846: an OMR draft in progress (`encode-T0002`).
- `blume-und-duft/`: Liszt, *Blume und Duft*: an OMR draft in progress (`encode-T0002`).
